sbc-emu: single-board computers in your browser

Each board is emulated down to the registers its Linux drivers use, by a pure-Rust emulator compiled to WebAssembly. Pick one and press Power on: it runs Linux on your computer, not on a server.

Raspberry Pi 4

Broadcom BCM2711 · 4× Cortex-A72 · 64-bit Arm

The Pi’s own kernel and a BusyBox shell on its HDMI screen, resumed from a snapshot. Type on its USB keyboard.

Open · 16 MB

Orange Pi One

Allwinner H3 · 4× Cortex-A7 · 32-bit Arm

Alpine Linux’s kernel booting from scratch, in about 5 seconds, to a shell on the serial console.

Open · 14 MB

Coming to this page: the ASUS Tinker Board, the Orange Pi 5 (eight cores) and the RISC-V Orange Pi RV. They already run in the native emulator, with their GPUs.
