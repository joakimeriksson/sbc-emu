sbc-emu Raspberry Pi 4B

Linux in an instruction-level AArch64 emulator, running in your browser

Your own SD card

Drop a Raspberry Pi 4 SD card image here, as you downloaded it (.img.xz, .img.gz or .img), or choose it. It stays on your computer: the emulator reads it where it is, and what the Pi writes is kept in memory until you close the tab.

  • Ubuntu Server 26.04 LTS: the 64-bit Raspberry Pi image from ubuntu.com/download/raspberry-pi (1.5 GB). Drop the .img.xz unopened: the page reads it without unpacking. The login prompt appears after about 6 minutes on a fast computer; give first-boot setup a few minutes more, then log in as ubuntu / ubuntu and choose a new password when asked.
  • Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) from raspberrypi.com (0.5 GB). The page unpacks it once into your browser’s storage (3 GB, about a minute), and reuses that on your next visit. On first boot it asks for your keyboard layout, then for a user name and password: click the screen and use your keyboard.
  • Server and Lite images, not desktops: the Pi here has 1 GB of RAM and runs at a fraction of a real one’s speed.

choose a system to boot

Click the screen to type on the Pi's USB keyboard (emulated DWC2 USB host + HID keyboard). The screen is the firmware framebuffer (simplefb, fbcon). Try cat /proc/cpuinfo or cat /dev/urandom > /dev/fb0.

LED · GPIO17

In the guest: gpioset -t0 GPIO17_LED=1, gpioget GPIO27_BUTTON, cat /sys/bus/iio/devices/iio:device0/in_temp_input, …/iio:device1/in_voltage0_raw.
