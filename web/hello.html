sbc-emu: a Raspberry Pi 4 in your browser

The Pi's own Linux kernel and a busybox shell, running on an emulated 4-core AArch64 machine compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing runs on a server.

Downloads a 15 MB snapshot of the Pi, already booted to its shell.

Click the screen to type on the Pi's USB keyboard, or use the box above. More demos, including Raspberry Pi OS with its 3D GPU.
